# Contributing (pre-release)

Thank you for your interest in `c2go-libc`.

> **External code contributions are not currently being accepted or merged.**

The project is preparing a dual-licensing and contributor-rights process. The
legal copyright owner, Contributor License Agreement (CLA), and authorized
acceptance workflow have not yet been finalized. Merging code before that work
is complete could prevent the project from offering consistent commercial
terms later.

## What is welcome now

Until a CLA process is published, contributors may provide non-code feedback
through whatever public issue channel is established for the eventual
repository, including:

- reproducible bug reports;
- compatibility observations;
- documentation corrections described in the reporter's own words; and
- links to relevant upstream specifications or source locations.

Do not paste third-party source code into an issue or contribution unless its
origin and license are known and the project has explicitly requested it.

## What is not accepted yet

Do not submit for merge:

- source-code patches;
- generated `.go` or `.s` files from an unverified toolchain;
- copied or adapted third-party code without complete provenance;
- changes that remove or replace upstream copyright/license notices; or
- contributions made on behalf of an employer without the necessary
  authorization.

A `Signed-off-by` line or DCO declaration alone is not the planned substitute
for the CLA required by this project's commercial dual-licensing model.

## Future contribution requirements

Before code contributions open, the project intends to publish:

1. the legal identity of the project copyright owner/licensor;
2. individual and corporate CLA terms;
3. an authorized CLA-signing and verification process;
4. source-format, generation, and clean-tree verification commands;
5. provenance requirements for adapted code;
6. a supported platform/Go-version test matrix; and
7. a security-reporting channel.

No contributor should sign or rely on a placeholder agreement. The final CLA
and contribution policy require qualified legal review.
