# Commercial licensing (pre-release)

[简体中文](COMMERCIAL-LICENSING.zh-CN.md)

> **Status: PRE-RELEASE — NO COMMERCIAL LICENSE IS GRANTED BY THIS FILE.**

The project intends to offer a separately executed commercial license for
original c2go material as an alternative to GNU AGPL-3.0-only. The selected
structure uses a natural person as licensor.

This repository does not yet publish:

- the individual's full legal identity and verified ownership record;
- an approved commercial-license agreement;
- pricing, support, warranty, indemnity, or SLA terms; or
- a commercial-licensing contact address.

Those details must not be invented or inferred from repository metadata. A
commercial license exists only when an authorized licensor and customer execute
an applicable agreement.

The agreement must identify the individual licensor and customer by their full
legal names, and identify the covered repository, tag/version, and commit hash.
It should expressly define the licensed rights, exclusivity, territory, term,
products, deployments, affiliates, redistribution/SaaS scope, fees, taxes,
termination, support, warranties, and third-party exclusions. Rights not
expressly granted remain reserved.

## GitHub Sponsors as payment channel

If the individual licensor is eligible to receive Sponsors payouts, an
executed agreement may designate GitHub Sponsors as a payment method. The
agreement, not the sponsorship action, grants rights. A tier selection,
payment, receipt, invoice, export, or transaction metadata alone grants no
commercial license.

A payment-linked tier should be truthfully described as a commercial-license
payment under a separately executed agreement, not as a no-benefit donation.
The agreement or an incorporated payment instruction must state the agreement
number, covered release/commit, amount and currency, gross-versus-net fee
treatment, taxes and withholding, refund/chargeback handling, paying account,
licensed legal entity, and the conditions for effectiveness. Transaction
metadata can carry a short agreement reference but is only supporting evidence.

Sponsors eligibility, supported regions, fees, and invoice rules can change;
confirm them before signing and include a fallback payment method when needed.
Never use false residency, identity, or third-party payout details to bypass
platform verification. Review the current
[GitHub Sponsors Additional Terms](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-sponsors-additional-terms)
and [GitHub Sponsors documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/sponsors/receiving-sponsorships-through-github-sponsors/about-github-sponsors-for-open-source-contributors)
before relying on this payment channel.

## Intended scope

A future commercial agreement may grant alternative rights only for material
that the licensor owns or is otherwise authorized to license. It may cover, for
example, verified original c2go runtime bridges, original glue, and original
build/generation code.

It cannot change or withdraw permissions already granted for:

- musl and musl-derived material;
- TRE, Sun/FreeBSD math, smoothsort, or other code bundled through musl;
- Apple Libc, FreeBSD, XNU, MinGW, Go, purego, or other third-party material;
  or
- any contribution for which the licensor did not obtain sufficient
  relicensing rights.

All third-party exclusions and notices must be attached to or incorporated by
reference into the final commercial agreement and distribution package.

## Public AGPL option

AGPL-3.0-only permits commercial use when its conditions are followed. Public
documentation must not say "commercial use requires payment." The accurate
message is:

> Original c2go material may be used under AGPL-3.0-only. Organizations that
> need different terms for that material may enter into a separate commercial
> agreement with the authorized licensor.

The application of the AGPL to a particular combination, generated artifact,
distribution, or network service depends on the actual facts. Marketing copy
must not promise that the AGPL automatically covers a customer's entire stack
or every file generated by a c2go tool.

## Work required before offering commercial terms

Before the project represents a commercial license as available, it must:

1. record and verify the selected individual's legal identity and ownership;
2. complete file-level provenance and ownership records;
3. adopt a contributor agreement that grants sufficient commercial
   relicensing rights;
4. have counsel draft and approve the agreement and third-party exclusions;
5. define the supported product, release versions, warranties, and support
   boundaries; and
6. publish an authorized contact channel.

Until then, this document records intent only and must not be treated as an
offer, contract, promise of availability, or grant of rights.
